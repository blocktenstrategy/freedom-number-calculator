A 4% withdrawal rate is the classic rule of thumb: your freedom number is 25 times your yearly shortfall. Lower is more conservative.

This calculator is an educational estimate, not financial advice. It assumes your other income is entered in today's money and rises with inflation, that contributions are made at the end of each month, and that returns are steady. Real markets are not.
